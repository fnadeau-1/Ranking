About

Rankings that belong to the wrestling community.

Montana Wrestling Rankings is a weekly, community-driven take on high school wrestling across the state. A trusted ranker sets each week's order by weight class — and then the community gets a real say.

Most ranking sites hand you a list and move on. Here, you push back: agree or disagree, drag out your own order, and watch everyone's picks average into a live People's Ranking that sits right next to the ranker's. It's the debate the wrestling room already has, made public and kept honest.

Anyone can read every ranking, current or past. Signing in with Google unlocks voting, commenting, and saving your own rankings.

⚖️

Fair by design

Votes, comments, and fan rankings are all validated on the server — no stuffing the ballot, no faking the consensus.

🔄

Live every week

New week, new board. Everything updates in real time as the ranker publishes and fans weigh in.

🥇

For the sport

Built to celebrate Montana wrestlers and give fans, families, and athletes a place to argue it out.
