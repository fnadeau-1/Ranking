About

Rankings that belong to the wrestling community.

Montana Wrestling Rankings is the weekly home for the state's official high school wrestling rankings. Each week, the head of Montana's rankings sets the authoritative order by weight class — that ranking is the record.

Alongside it, the community gets a real say. Fans agree or disagree, build their own order from the same wrestlers, and watch everyone's picks average into a live People's Ranking shown next to the official one. The official board leads; the people's verdict runs beside it.

Anyone can read every ranking, current or past. Signing in with Google unlocks voting, commenting, and saving your own rankings.

Fair by design

Votes, comments, and fan rankings are all validated on the server — no stuffing the ballot, no faking the consensus.

Live every week

New week, new official board. Everything updates in real time as rankings publish and fans weigh in.

For the sport

Built to recognize Montana wrestlers and give fans, families, and athletes a place to make their case.
