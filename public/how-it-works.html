The rundown

How it works

Reading the rankings is open to everyone. To vote, comment, or build your own ranking, you sign in with Google — that's it.

1

The head of Montana's rankings sets the official board

Each week, the state's head rankings authority publishes the official order for every weight class. That's the record — the authoritative ranking everything else is measured against.

2

You agree or disagree

One tap registers whether you back the official ranking or not. The agree/disagree tally is public and updates live as votes come in.

3

You build your own ranking

Drag the exact same wrestlers into the order you'd run. You can't add or remove anyone — it's purely your call on who beats who.

4

The People's Ranking forms

Every saved fan ranking averages together into a live consensus. Flip the toggle on any ranking to see it, complete with arrows showing how far the crowd moved each wrestler from the official spot.

5

You talk it out

Comments are open on the current week's ranking — up to 10 per person, per week, to keep it real. Past weeks stay fully readable but their threads are closed.
