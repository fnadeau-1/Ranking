Privacy & Data

What we collect, and why.

Montana Wrestling Rankings keeps data collection to the minimum needed to run the site. You can read every ranking without signing in or sharing anything. This page explains what happens when you do sign in and take part.

Reading the site

Browsing rankings, team standings, and comments requires no account and collects no personal information from you beyond the standard technical logs any website receives (such as your IP address and browser type, kept by our hosting provider, Google Firebase, for security and reliability).

When you sign in with Google

Signing in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. When you sign in we receive and store:

  • your name and profile photo from your Google account,
  • your email address and whether it is verified,
  • a unique account identifier (your user ID).

We use this only to identify you on the site, attribute your votes and comments, and gate features that require a verified email.

What you create

  • Votes — when you agree or disagree with a ranking, we store your account ID, the ranking voted on, your choice, and the time.
  • Comments — the text you post, your display name, your account ID, and the time. Comments are public.
  • Your rankings — if you build your own order, we store that order tied to your account ID so it can roll into the People's Ranking.

Spam and abuse prevention

The site uses Google reCAPTCHA and Firebase App Check to block automated abuse. reCAPTCHA collects hardware and software information and sends it to Google for analysis, subject to Google's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties except the infrastructure providers (Google Firebase and reCAPTCHA) needed to operate the site.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time from the Settings page. To request deletion of your account data (votes, comments, and saved rankings), contact the site operator and we'll remove it. You can always keep using the site anonymously by not signing in.

This notice may be updated as the site evolves. Last updated when the Teams and Settings features were added.
